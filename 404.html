Research

B.Sc. thesis · 2025–2026 · Enam Medical College Hospital

Reading paper ECGs from a smartphone photograph

On 240 held-out records, opened once after the labels were fixed, a plain ConvNeXt-Tiny reached a macro AUROC of 0.923 (95% CI 0.895–0.946); arrhythmia and conduction block remain the weak spot (sensitivity 0.42).

Paper 1 releases the dataset (1,191 photographs, cardiologist labels) and a pre-specified evaluation protocol with external tests in Germany, Pakistan and the USA. Paper 2 generates a structured written report from the same photograph.

Manuscript in preparation · target IEEE JBHI

Classifier-Gated Structured Reports From Smartphone Photographs of Printed 12-Lead Electrocardiograms

M. R. Islam, M. A. Hossain, M. A. Moni (author order to be confirmed)

A frozen system writes four-line cardiologist-style reports from the photo without losing diagnostic accuracy, and adds more correct findings than a template.

Synthetic example, not a patient
Rate: 112/min (sinus tachycardia)
Findings: Right bundle-branch block; ST depression V4-V6
Impression: Abnormal ECG - Arrhythmia/block, Ischaemia, Rate abnormality
Advice: Clinical correlation; consider echocardiography
Confidence: high (no referral)

Internal test set, 240 photographs (opened once for this paper)

  • Impression macro-F1: report 0.738 vs template 0.734
    Difference +0.005 (-0.015 to +0.027): non-inferior (pre-specified)
  • Statement F1: report 0.551 vs template 0.497
    Difference +0.054 (+0.006 to +0.103): more correct findings
  • Heart-rate error: 1.9 beats/min (mean absolute)
  • PULSE-7B (7-billion-parameter ECG vision-language model, zero-shot): impression macro-F1 0.443
  • Referral: about 20% of photographs are referred to a human by an uncertainty rule fixed on development data

Interval plots: difference from template, 95% CI; the vertical line marks zero.

External validation

Tested beyond one hospital

A classifier trained only on phone photographs from Dhaka, frozen with no retraining, was scored on phone photographs of public ECGs from three other countries. Macro AUROC ranged from 0.804 to 0.907.

Referral to a human rose from 20% at home to 41-45% abroad.

Label sets differ between datasets, so this is not a head-to-head comparison.

Status: internal result, paper in preparation

Independent study

Can vision-language models read paper ECGs?

A small model read lead labels well on a clean render, a hand-held phone photo cost it some of that, and it did not read rhythm at all: it answered "regular" 80 of 80 times.

Fat-tissue thermogenesis genes in mice after spaceflight

In RT-qPCR of 89 thermogenesis genes from gonadal white adipose tissue (OSD-970; 16 female C57BL/6J mice, 37 days on the ISS, RR-1), Ucp1 was up 12.21-fold (ΔΔCt -3.61, p = 0.0167). Small sample: 16 mice; exploratory.
